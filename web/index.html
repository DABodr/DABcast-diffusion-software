PFT / FEC protects EDI over UDP against lost packets (usual for commercial modulators, not needed over TCP); it applies to every EDI output. The TIST offset delays the timestamps so that transmitters reading them (EDI, EasyDAB V2) can fill their buffer (EasyDAB V2: 2 s; 0 otherwise). The ETI monitoring output (Player / Dablin) is always kept.

        

        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      


      

        

        

        
        
      

    

  

  
    

      

        

          
Audio levels

          
Peaks measured by odr-dabmux over a sliding window (measured while this page is open)

        

        
      

      

        
        
        
        

        
      

      

      
Typical peak = median of the peaks (silence ignored). Max = highest peak measured; in red above -3 dBFS: AAC encoding raises the decoded peaks by 1-3 dB, so higher peaks can crackle in receivers — the suggestion then lowers the gain, unless they are isolated (fewer than 3 samples above -3 dBFS and no encoder clipping): those only prevent a boost. CLIP = odr-audioenc reported clipping in the window: at least -2 dB is suggested. A longer analysis window (15-30 min, songs + ads + jingles) gives a more reliable suggestion. A boost is limited to +6 dB per step: apply, then measure again. Peaks are not perceived loudness: two stations with the same peaks can sound different depending on their processing — use the suggestion as a starting point. Apply saves the gain and restarts only that service's encoder (2–3 s gap on that service, the multiplex keeps running).
